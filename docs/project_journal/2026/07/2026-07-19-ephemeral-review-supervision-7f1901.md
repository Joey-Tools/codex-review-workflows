# Lightweight Ephemeral Independent Codex Review

## Summary

- Independent Codex PR review now uses a clean detached worktree and a fresh `codex exec --ephemeral` process while preserving the normal Codex user environment and tracked project instructions.
- A lightweight process-group supervisor replaces the mandatory container/job/cgroup boundary for this lane.
- Diagnostic log health and process cleanup health no longer invalidate a separately sealed review artifact.

## Current State

- `independent-codex-pr-review` starts a new read-only ephemeral Codex process and never resumes an earlier session or creates a temporary `CODEX_HOME`.
- Normal user configuration, Rules, MCP servers, Plugins, and tracked `AGENTS.md` / `.codex/**` instructions remain available to the reviewer. The exact frozen diff still includes tracked instruction changes.
- The reviewer leader owns a recorded PGID. Deadline or hard-cap termination uses bounded `TERM` / grace / `KILL`; descendant inventory and cross-`setsid` containment are intentionally not required for this accepted-risk lane.
- Stdout and stderr rotate at 4 MiB of uncompressed input into ordered lossless archives. Each stream admits at most 128 MiB, and all active, temporary, and archived process logs share a 256 MiB on-disk hard limit.
- The final artifact remains separately capped at 64 KiB. `--output-last-message` must target a fresh private FIFO whose supervised blocking reader holds no dummy writer, writes a separate ordinary artifact, and seals it only after true EOF. The reader then file-`fsync`s, atomically publishes without replacement, directory-`fsync`s, and completes no-follow identity/digest/exact-byte readback before any `clean` or `findings` terminal tuple can become durable.
- A diagnostic reader that misses EOF is both `logs-truncated` and `cleanup-warning`. Other safe residual-process evidence is also `cleanup-warning`. Either may accompany a valid `clean` or `findings` artifact. An incomplete final transport remains `inconclusive`.
- Clean no-findings logs are removed only when cleanup is clean and both diagnostic readers reached EOF. Bounded compressed logs and paths that may still be open are retained for cleanup/logging warnings, findings, blockers, inconclusive attempts, or recovery handoffs.
- All active and retained process artifacts share one private 512 MiB retention-root cap. An interprocess root lock and one generation/digest-chained authoritative attempt-state record make accounting, reclaim, the durable 257 MiB process charge, filesystem-grouped checkout charges, launch phase, and settlement one recoverable transaction. Physical admission maps process headroom to the retention-root filesystem and combines it with checkout-root and Git-admin headroom when identities match, while the 512 MiB process and 1 GiB checkout logical caps remain separate. A phase helper holds a duplicate of the same BSD `flock` open-file-description through publication and exit, preventing a stale writer after parent death; uncertain or orphaned charges remain fully reserved.
- Evidence moves monotonically through `active/unsafe`, `held`, `released`, and `reclaimed`. Only safely closed evidence explicitly released as `resolved` or `handoff-complete` is reclaimable, and its 7-day TTL starts at `released_at`; unresolved or possibly open evidence never expires by age.
- The detached checkout uses `worktree add --no-checkout`, a raw `read-tree` / `cat-file` materializer, a dedicated checkout-worker PGID, and killable phase-commit helpers so blocking data or state `fsync` cannot evade the 10-minute supervision deadline. Before consuming blobs it rejects filesystem-native case/Unicode aliases through an exclusive no-follow skeleton with ordinary placeholders for regular and symlink leaves; regular files preserve the Git `100644`/`100755` executable-bit mapping, bounded symlink blobs are installed only by verified atomic exchange, and every materialized path is checked for tracked external conversion. Its strict raw-input limits feed a separate 1 GiB conservative checkout-accounting admission cap; that value is not represented as an OS quota. It executes no checkout hook, filter, LFS download, or working-tree attribute conversion.
- Targeted mixed-state cleanup uses a reservation-bound external descendant manifest charged to the checkout ledger. Its accounting covers the checkout root, leaves, unique parent directories and full raw parent paths, synthetic entries, the Git-registration root and bounded descendants, temporary and published manifest copies, and the sibling control namespace. Parent aggregation keeps only the previous Git-ordered raw path, runs the complete zero-parent projection before consuming the stream, and reruns the same overflow-checked checkout/filesystem projection at every newly observed parent slash; a proved limit stops consumption before the next byte. Successful EOF must match the authenticated entry count.
- The helper-backed offline gate retains its already-preflighted frozen workspace through independent-lane input sealing. Its `preflight.json.primary_diff` now attests the workspace-relative path, length, and SHA-256 of the exact scanned `review.diff`, alongside the frozen range. Synchronous fallback eligibility rejects missing, malformed, mismatched, oversized, identity-drifted, or size-drifted evidence using bounded control reads and no-follow metadata only; it intentionally does not hash up to 128 MiB in `status()`. `--keep-workspace` supplies initial availability, while a shared `cleanup.lock` lease and already-open no-follow source FD provide race-safe custody against concurrent explicit cleanup. The actual fallback consumer or independent checkout must stream and verify the complete SHA-256 under its own supervised deadline. The independent copy runs only inside the 10-minute checkout deadline, seals `.codex-review/review.diff` into the charged synthetic control manifest, and releases the source lease only after durable readback. The reviewer receives no outside-workspace directory or descriptor.
- The independent prompt is preflighted once in bounded memory and sealed by length and SHA-256. No prompt artifact exists before admission; the durable `reserved` record binds its intended path and digest before exclusive no-follow publication inside the 257 MiB envelope. The exact bytes are passed as the only positional prompt. Reviewer stdin is `/dev/null`; descriptors above fd 2 are closed, reviewer-launch descriptors are closed in every cleanup path, and the final FIFO is passed only by pathname to a blocking-reader handshake with no dummy writer. After the first writer joins, the reader verifies and durably unlinks the FIFO name before reading payload, so later writers cannot reopen it and true EOF seals the original writer set.
- Each ephemeral invocation directly passes byte-stable argv overrides for table-valued worktree trust, `.git` as the project-root marker, `features.hooks=false`, and `notify=[]` without mutating the user's configuration. Dotted project keys remain forbidden because dots in absolute paths are parsed as key separators. Normal local and organization-managed configuration is part of the explicitly trusted reviewer environment rather than a separately pinned isolation layer; a higher-priority managed policy may prevail. The lane performs no JWT parsing, executable allowlist, feature pre-probe, app-server configuration handshake, or secondary `CODEX_HOME`. `codex debug prompt-input` remains prohibited because it starts an extra Session and configured MCP startup instead of validating the actual reviewer invocation.
- Prelaunch state is durable in one single-source record from reservation through worktree creation, validation, spawn intent, and launch. A fixed PID-first native trampoline plus owned-helper post-exec ready records keep every child observable. After prompt publication but while the attempt is still `reserved`, one small native attempt supervisor receives the exact prompt bytes and ownership through `pending -> accepted -> outer publication quiescence -> authenticated start ACK -> complete`; no checkout worker, phase helper, FIFO reader, or reviewer may start before the durable complete generation. Before complete, outer EOF, malformed control, or deadline cancellation makes the supervisor reap its helpers, close its publication/source-cleanup leases, and exit so recovery cannot be permanently lock-starved. The supervisor then becomes every later child's common direct parent and sole state writer. It owns separate 30-second source-custody/ownership-handoff, 10-minute checkout, 30-second reader/reviewer launch, and 30-minute reviewer deadlines. Codex CLOEXEC EOF counts as exec only while the anchored child is still verified live. `prelaunch-aborted` is reserved for proven no-Codex paths; ambiguous exec or failed `launched` persistence remains `launch-uncertain`. An exhaustive matrix covers same-boot supervisor-owner closure and verified boot-change exact settlement for every nonterminal phase without coupling process settlement to checkout cleanup.
- A clean terminal review re-proves exact ownership, force-removes its raw detached checkout in the sanitized Git environment, and verifies that both its path and Git registration disappear. An authenticated mixed checkout/registration state may use bounded descriptor-relative cleanup only while the original owner or a live guardian continuously retains the exact parent/root descriptors and lock lease. If that custody chain dies mid-removal, recovery records `manual-recovery-required`, keeps the non-TTL `retained-worktree` and full checkout charge, and performs no successor unlink. Any other removal uncertainty likewise remains `blocked-worktree-capacity` and prevents a later independent review from creating another checkout.
- The helper-backed Codex lane is unchanged and continues to require persisted-rollout model and effort verification. The independent ephemeral lane records requested model/effort and any observed effective values; a difference is reportable but is not itself a blocker because trusted higher-priority managed policy may prevail. Absent effective metadata is reported as unobserved because no rollout is persisted.

## Accepted Tradeoff

- Tracked project instructions and normal user MCP/Plugin configuration can influence the reviewer. The review prompt forbids state-changing external actions, but this is not a prompt-injection or tool-isolation boundary; a stricter repo-local policy may require trusted-baseline instructions or an allowlist.
- The invocation requests `features.hooks=false` and `notify=[]`, including suppression of user hooks and legacy notify. This aims to prevent the linked-worktree loader from substituting main-checkout project hooks while normal user configuration, Rules, MCP servers, Plugins, tracked instructions, and other tracked project configuration remain active; it is not an independently attested effective-config proof.
- Local and organization-managed Codex configuration is intentionally trusted input to the reviewer. This avoids container-like runtime pinning and app-server/cloud-cache probes, but it also means a higher-priority managed policy can supersede the requested hook, root, model, effort, or sandbox values. An explicit configuration conflict before reviewer execution is `blocked` / `not-run`; any observed hook or notify execution makes the attempt `inconclusive`.
- A reviewer descendant that creates a new session or process group may escape PGID cleanup. This is an accepted low-probability resource leak risk for the independent Codex gate; the clean worktree, deadline, output caps, and parent-owned final-artifact transport remain bounded.
- Timeout/limit cleanup signals the PGID only while the live or unreaped leader keeps its PID unavailable for reuse. Ordinary completion is first observed without reap; bounded drain and one PGID cleanup pass complete while that identity anchor remains. After reap, the supervisor never signals a bare numeric PGID.
- Current Codex JSONL does not expose model/effort, and `--ephemeral` intentionally persists no rollout. The lane therefore records requested values, reports future observed differences without failing solely on that basis, and reports effective values as unobserved when absent without claiming silent-substitution detection.
- When cleanup cannot be proven, retain paths that may still be open and report the warning instead of deleting them or downgrading an already sealed artifact. A possibly in-use detached worktree is likewise retained, but it blocks the next independent review until exact cleanup succeeds.
- The ordinary attempt supervisor is not a kernel containment boundary. Its unexpected crash, an incomplete owner handoff, or an escaped descendant whose closure cannot be proven keeps the full process reservation fail-closed; a verified boot change remains the final reconciliation path.
- The lightweight lane assumes cooperative same-UID namespace behavior during targeted cleanup. Its descriptor, manifest, and drift checks are not protection against a malicious same-UID process that races the final identity check and unlink; adding that boundary would require the heavier containment profile deliberately excluded here. Observed drift fails closed, and loss of the continuous descriptor-custody chain requires manual recovery without changing an independently sealed review result.
