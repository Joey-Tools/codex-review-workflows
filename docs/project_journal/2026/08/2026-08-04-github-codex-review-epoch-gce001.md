## Decision Contract

- Applicable current-head or proved-ancestor findings and unresolved authoritative threads block. Proved nonancestor evidence is excluded before reduction. Unknown ancestry is pending only while bounded acquisition can recover, then becomes an explicit error.
- A carrier-valid, final-stable current-head clean may supersede only an older same-head or ancestor threadless finding. It cannot close an unresolved thread. Ancestor-bound clean alone remains pending; hashless issue-comment clean has no watermark substitute.
- Ancestor-bound clean, valid progress, unknown ancestry, and transient incomplete acquisition may remain `PENDING` only while every applicable local acquisition or ancestry budget is unexhausted and the epoch is still before the overall 7,200-second deadline. Exhausting the applicable local budget maps to `ERROR`. At or after the overall deadline, any still-budgeted or other incomplete acquisition maps to overall-timeout `FAILURE` before late `PASS`; only with no incomplete acquisition may an actual complete, final-stable, exact-current-head late terminal clean plus canonical Action `success` become `PASS`. Deterministic malformed evidence errors without authorizing a blind POST. `eyes` retains only its existing ACK/liveness role and does not reset the ACK, result, or overall deadlines. `+1` has no `PASS` or ACK authority.
- The Action producer status projection is closed: an accepted current-head clean plus canonical success maps to `success` only through the ordered reducer above; a proved blocking finding maps to `failure`; only bounded pre-deadline states map to `pending`; deterministic malformed or locally exhausted unknown/transient state maps to `error`; and remaining incomplete or nonterminal state at 7,200 seconds maps to `failure` before the narrow late-clean arm.
- The reducer's sole authority is one parent-owned sealed composite coordinate binding the sixteen-role required-status membership, final provider validation, immutable epoch first-attempt clock, and current marker/attempt state. The six report planes are independent outputs rather than separate reducer authorities; raw probes, caller dictionaries, independent provider/status projections, marker objects, and standalone clock values cannot replace that coordinate.
- Provider and required-Action acquisition type-sensitively loads the epoch machine's exact configuration into one parent-owned composed-operation budget ledger covering the complete REST/GraphQL, run/attempt, artifact/archive/workflow, release/asset/tag/provenance, source/Action tree, and raw-resource graph. Each carried `resource_budget` equals that configuration. Counters, retained UTF-8 bytes, and deadline are cumulative, while only the body ceiling is per body. No initial/final capture, candidate/sibling evaluation, caller, release, or test input may reset, split, refund, borrow, override, or reseal the ledger.

- Release discovery derives the complete ordered candidate vector as `valid`, `proved-incompatible`, or `malformed-or-incomplete-error`. Only complete, well-typed, authenticated, internally unambiguous evidence—including a valid OpenPGP signature by one unambiguous nontrusted primary signer or zero candidate-local `W` matches—that proves an admission predicate false is `proved-incompatible`; one `W` match is eligible, while more than one is malformed. Missing, invalid, ambiguous, or unverifiable tag/signature evidence and integrity/cross-binding contradictions make the whole admission `ERROR`. Otherwise exclude only proved-incompatible candidates and require exactly one valid release.
